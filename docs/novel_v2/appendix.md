## Appendix A: Glossary of Terminology

**Tensor Fluid (텐서 유체)**
The conceptualization of the vacuum of space not as an empty void, but as a compressible, dynamic fluid governed by complex topological and hydrodynamic principles.

**Cosmic Shear / Phase-Transition Wave (우주적 전단 / 상전이 파동)**
A macroscopic topological fracture propagating through the spatial metric, capable of unzipping the local geometry of the universe and altering the fundamental constants of gravity and distance.

**Matched Singularity Cancellation / Exact Cancellation (정합 특이점 상쇄 / 완전 상쇄)**
A topological regularization technique pioneered by Ian Yoo. It involves generating an artificial, opposing geometric phase rotation ($C_s$) to perfectly match the singular geometry of a collapsing spatial defect ($Q_s$), thereby algebraically neutralizing the divergence before the physical limit is reached.

**Epsilon Regularization (엡실론 정규화)**
An orthodox, mathematically conservative patch championed by Professor Arthur Sterling. It utilizes an arbitrary scalar constant ($\epsilon$) to artificially cap unphysical infinities in effective field models, effectively hiding underlying structural flaws in the spatial metric.

**Comoving Metric Neighborhood (동행 계량 이웃)**
A localized bubble of spacetime that translates cohesively across macroscopic distances. Objects within this bubble preserve their internal inertial reference frames and experience zero acceleration or kinetic friction, despite bypassing vast Euclidean distances.

**Standing Wave Node (정상파 마디)**
A localized coordinate within the tensor fluid experiencing destructive interference, where the vibrational amplitude drops to zero. This effectively nullifies standard gravitational vectors, allowing matter to decouple from planetary mass.

## Appendix B: Key Governing Equations

**1. Matched Singularity Cancellation Limit (정합 특이점 상쇄 극한)**
The fundamental algebraic principle of neutralizing a spatial tear by matching its divergent geometry:
$$ \lim_{r \to 0} [Q_s(r) + C_s(r)] = q_0 + c_0 \rightarrow 0 $$

**2. Tensor Fluid Continuity Equation (텐서 유체 연속 방정식)**
Adapted from classical hydrodynamics, defining the conservation of mass-energy within the compressible spatial vacuum:
$$ \frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\rho v) = 0 $$

**3. Topological Phase Rotation (위상학적 위상 회전)**
The quantum geometric rotation induced to manipulate the local tensor field:
$$ S = \hbar\theta $$

**4. Bianchi Identities & Conservation (비앙키 항등식 및 보존칙)**
The geometric constraint demanding that the stress-energy tensor remains locally conserved, ensuring that topological energy cannot be administratively deleted:
$$ \nabla_\mu \tilde{V}^{\mu\nu} = 0 $$
